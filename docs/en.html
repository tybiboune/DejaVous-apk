WHAT’S NEW · VERSION 1.29

Every update
gives you a reason to replay.

Déjà Vous keeps growing: questions chosen for your story, a background that lives, a Spicy mode that catches fire and plenty of small touches made for couples.

01 /

Cards that fit your story

At first launch, tell the game how long you have known each other: getting to know each other, about a year, or over five years. It picks the questions itself: no wedding memories between strangers, no “favourite colour” after ten years. At least 100 cards per game and intensity for every stage.

02 /

A background that truly lives

More coloured halos drifting gently, and a glow 30% stronger in light mode. Dark mode is unchanged.

03 /

Spicy mode catches fire

Switch Spicy mode on: shining red and yellow particles fly up from the switch.

04 /

A vault you can tidy

Clear one specific tab of the vault (answers, vouchers, history, truths) without touching the others. Switching tabs now only animates what changes.

05 /

Quick games: back where you were

Closing a quick game takes you back to the quick-games list, not the main menu.

06 /

150+ cards at every level

Same Brain, Fusion, Two Truths, Rapid Fire and Do You Know Me? each hold 150 cards or more at every intensity, from the tenderest to the hottest. Over 9,000 pieces of content, no duplicates.

07 /

Resume the two-phone game

A phone leaves the app, restarts or loses Bluetooth? The game in progress picks up exactly where it was. Only the “quit” button ends it for good.

08 /

A vault that survives everything

Turn on the optional Google backup: your vault is copied into a hidden folder of your Google Drive that only Déjà Vous can read. New phone: sign in again and everything is back. No code to remember.
