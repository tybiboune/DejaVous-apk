    06 / BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

    The seven questions
    everyone asks.

    How do I install it?

    Tap “Download”, open the file, and allow installation from your browser when Android asks. Three taps, one minute. The file is signed and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with every version.

    Is it really free?

    The download is free and includes the Discovery version: a real selection of cards and games, with no account and no ads. The full game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

    What if we don’t like it?

    Refunded within 14 days, no questions asked: just reply to the purchase e-mail. The code is then switched off on your phones.

    What happens to our answers?

    They stay on your phones. No account, no ads, no telemetry. Google backup is optional and encrypted with a password only you know.

    Do we need two phones?

    No. Pass one screen back and forth, or each play on your own over Bluetooth, side by side.

    How many phones does one purchase cover?

    One purchase unlocks the full game on 2 phones at most: yours and your partner’s, even with different Google accounts. An Internet connection is needed the first time each phone is activated; after that everything works offline. New phone? The app offers to replace the old one, once a month.

    Which edition is offered here?

    Unleashed, the direct Android edition. A separate Google Play edition with adapted content is planned; availability and approval are not guaranteed. Their prices and content differ. See the editions.

    Coloured halos drifting gently, a glow 30% stronger in light mode. The animation runs on the graphics processor: the app starts faster and stays smooth, even on a modest phone.

    06 /

    Switch Spicy mode on: shining red and yellow particles fly up from the switch while the colours change gradually.

    07 /

    A vault you can tidy

    Clear one specific tab of the vault (answers, vouchers, history, truths) without touching the others. Switching tabs now only animates what changes.

    Is your partner taking their time? A button shakes their screen and pops a frosted-glass sticker onto it: speech bubble, star, heart, cloud, ticket… a different shape every time.

    Same Brain, Fusion, Two Truths, Rapid Fire and Do You Know Me? each hold 150 cards or more at every intensity, from the tenderest to the hottest. Over 11,000 pieces of content, no duplicates.

    Resume the two-phone game

    A phone leaves the app, restarts or loses Bluetooth? The game in progress picks up exactly where it was. Only the “quit” button ends it for good.

    11 /

    A vault that survives everything

    Turn on the optional Google backup: your vault is encrypted with a password you choose, then copied into a hidden folder of your Google Drive. Without that password nobody can read it, not even us. New phone: sign in again, enter it, and everything is back. Forgot it? Set a new one from a phone that still has the backup.
