04 / BEFORE YOU START

The questions
you were about to ask.

How do I install it?

On Google Play: tap “Install.” For the direct edition: tap “Download,” open the file and allow installation from your browser when Android asks. The file is signed, and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with every version. Outside the Play Store, a Play Protect warning is normal: here’s what to do.

Is it really free?

The download is, in both editions. It includes the Discovery version: a real selection of cards in every game and at each of its levels, matched to how long you’ve been together, plus one card from the full game every day. No account, no ads. The full game is something you pay for once, not a subscription.

Which edition is offered here?

Two: Google Play, the adapted edition, at €24.90 (in-app purchase through Google Play), and Unleashed, the direct edition, at €34.90 (card payment through Stripe). See the editions.

What is the difference?

Google Play: 12,000+ gentler cards, no Spicy mode. Unleashed: 18,000+ cards, all five levels, Spicy mode and new cards online. The game and its rules are the same.

We don’t like it. Now what?

Unleashed: reply to the purchase e-mail within 14 days and you get your money back, no questions asked. Google Play: refunds follow Google Play’s rules.

What happens to our answers?

They stay on your phones. No account, no ads, no telemetry. Only The Thread and the evenings you give travel, in a link you send yourselves, without going through our servers. Google backup is optional, and encrypted with a password only you know.

Do we need two phones?

No. One screen you pass back and forth is enough. With two phones, you play side by side over Bluetooth; when you’re apart, you send each other The Thread by message.

How many phones does one purchase cover?

One purchase unlocks the full game on 2 phones at most: yours and your partner’s, even with different Google accounts. Each phone needs an Internet connection the first time it’s activated, then at least every 45 days to confirm the purchase; in between, everything works offline. New phone? Paste your code on it: the app offers to free up the old one (once every 30 days).
