04 / BEFORE YOU DIVE IN

The questions
everyone asks.

How do I install it?

On Google Play: tap “Install”. Direct edition: tap “Download”, open the file and allow installation from your browser when Android asks. The file is signed and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with every version.

Is it really free?

The download is free in both editions and includes the Discovery version: a real selection of cards in every game and at each of its levels, matched to how long you have been together, plus one card from the full game every day. No account, no ads. The full game is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

Which edition is offered here?

Two: Google Play, the adapted edition at €24.90 (in-app purchase through Google Play), and Unleashed, the direct edition at €34.90 (card payment through Stripe). See the editions.

What is the difference?

Google Play: 12,000+ gentler cards, no Spicy mode. Unleashed: 18,000+ cards, all five levels, Spicy mode and new cards online. Same game, same rules.

What if we don’t like it?

Unleashed: refunded within 14 days, no questions asked: reply to the purchase e-mail. Google Play: refunds follow Google Play’s rules.

What happens to our answers?

They stay on your phones. No account, no ads, no telemetry. Only The Thread and the evenings you offer travel, in a link you send yourselves, without going through our servers. Google backup is optional and encrypted with a password only you know.

Do we need two phones?

No. Pass one screen back and forth, each play on your own over Bluetooth, side by side, or, when apart, send each other The Thread by message.

How many phones does one purchase cover?

One purchase unlocks the full game on 2 phones at most: yours and your partner’s, even with different Google accounts. An Internet connection is needed the first time each phone is activated, then at least every two weeks to confirm the purchase; in between everything works offline. New phone? The app offers to replace the old one, once a month.
