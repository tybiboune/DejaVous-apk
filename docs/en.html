06 / BEFORE YOU START

The questions
you were about to ask.

How do I install it?

From Google Play, like any other app: tap “Install.”From Google Play, as soon as it’s there, which is soon. In the meantime, the demo plays right here, nothing to install.

Is it really free?

Discovery is: cards in every game, at every level, matched to how long you’ve been together, plus one card from the full game a day. The full game costs €24.90, once, paid in the app through Google Play. No subscription.

We don’t like it. Now what?

The purchase goes through Google Play, and Google Play handles refunds, under its own rules. Its help page explains how.

Is it racy?

Romantic, no more. Kisses, seduction and declarations, never explicit. The game is for adults, but you could read it on a train without blushing. Quietly, though.

What happens to our answers?

They stay on your phones, with no telemetry. Only The Thread and the evenings you give travel, in a link you send yourselves, without going through our servers. Google backup is optional and encrypted with a password only you know.

Do we need two phones?

No, one screen you pass around is enough. With two phones, play over Bluetooth; apart, use The Thread.

What if we change phones?

The full game bought on Google Play follows your Google account. The Book of us travels with the Google backup, if you turned it on.
