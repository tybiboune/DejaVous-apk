Frequently asked questions

Why an extra payment if I already bought on Google Play?

Your Play purchase covers the toned-down version. Unleashed is another app, with thousands more cards that Google cannot host. Hence an extra, kept deliberately small: €9.90.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. The same code unlocks the full game on two phones, yours and your partner’s, even with different Google accounts. And to play together over Bluetooth, it’s enough for one of the two to have it.

Can I play over Bluetooth with someone on the Google Play version?

No, the two editions don’t have the same cards. Install the same one on both phones.

New phone, or reinstalled the app?

Reinstall Unleashed and paste the same code. One purchase covers 2 phones at most, yours and your partner’s. If you switch devices, the app offers to free the old one, once every 30 days. Lost the code? “Find my code”, further up.

What happens if I get a refund?

The code is canceled. The app notices as soon as it reaches our server, and goes back to the Discovery version. As long as it stays offline, nothing changes.

Payment pending, and still no code?

Some payment methods take their time to confirm. The code goes out on its own once they do, and the thank-you page shows it. Otherwise, “Find my code”.

I paid twice.

It happens, and each payment created its own code. Write to us at contact@dejanous.love: we refund the duplicate.

What do you know about me?

If you buy: your e-mail address, your order (product, language, date, Stripe payment references) and your code. Stripe sends us your e-mail and the payment status, never your card number. For the two-phone limit: a pseudonymous ID for each activated phone, with its activation date. The paid texts carry an invisible mark linked to your license.

There is no account: your answers, your first names and “The Book of us” stay on your phone. Google backup is optional: encrypted on your phone with a password you choose, it stays in your own Google Drive, and we have no access to it.

The site sets no cookies and counts a few events anonymously. The purchase and code-resend forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots. All the details, including how long data is kept: privacy policy.

Is the app safe?

It is signed, and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with each version, so you can check. Its updates come from this site and nowhere else.

What if Google Play is down when I upgrade?

Nothing is charged: we refuse rather than guess. Try again later.
