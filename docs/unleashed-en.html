Frequently asked questions

Why an extra payment if I already bought on Google Play?

Because your Play purchase covers the toned-down version. Unleashed is another app, with thousands more cards that Google cannot host. The extra is deliberately small: €9.90.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. One purchase unlocks the full game on two phones: yours and your partner’s, with the same code, even with different Google accounts. And to play together over Bluetooth, only one of the two phones even needs the full game.

Can I play over Bluetooth with someone on the Google Play version?

No: the cards differ between editions. Install the same edition on both phones.

New phone, or reinstalled the app? What now?

Reinstall Unleashed and paste the same code. One purchase works on 2 phones at most: yours and your partner’s (changing phones? the app offers to free the old one, once every 30 days). Lost it? “Find my code” above.

What happens if I get a refund?

The code is canceled: the app notices as soon as it can reach our server and goes back to the Discovery version. Offline, nothing changes.

My payment is pending and nothing has arrived?

Some payment methods confirm with a delay. The code is sent automatically once confirmed, and the thank-you page shows it. Otherwise: “Find my code”.

I paid twice.

One purchase = one code. Write to us at contact@dejanous.love: we refund the duplicate.

What do you know about me?

If you buy: your e-mail address, your order (product, language, date, Stripe payment references) and your code. Stripe sends us your e-mail and the payment status, never your card number. For the two-phone limit: a pseudonymous ID for each activated phone, with its activation date. The paid texts carry an invisible mark linked to your license.

There is no account: your answers, your first names and “The Book of us” stay on your phone. Google backup is optional: encrypted on your phone with a password you choose, it stays in your own Google Drive, and we have no access to it.

The site sets no cookies and counts a few events anonymously. The purchase and code-resend forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots. All the details, including how long data is kept: privacy policy.

Is the app safe?

It is signed, and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with each version. It updates from this site only.

What if Google Play is down when I upgrade?

Nothing is charged: we refuse rather than guess. Try again later.
