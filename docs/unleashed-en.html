Frequently asked questions

Why an extra payment if I already bought on Google Play?

Because your Play purchase covers the toned-down version. Unleashed is another app, with thousands more cards that Google cannot host. The extra is deliberately small: €9.90.

Does my partner have to pay too?

No. On two phones it is enough that one has the full game: the other can use the free “Discovery” version of the same edition (direct download).

Can I play over Bluetooth with someone on the Google Play version?

No: the cards differ between editions. Install the same edition on both phones.

I changed phone or reinstalled. What now?

Reinstall Unleashed and paste the same code. It works offline and on all your phones. Lost it? “Find my code” above.

What if I am refunded?

The code is cancelled: the app notices as soon as it can reach our server and goes back to the Discovery version. Offline, nothing changes.

My payment is pending, nothing received?

Some payment methods confirm with a delay. The code is sent automatically once confirmed, and the thank-you page shows it. Otherwise: “Find my code”.

I paid twice.

One purchase = one code. If you pay twice for the same Google Play order you get the same code and we refund the duplicate.

What do you know about me?

Your e-mail and your order, nothing else. The game has no account and collects no answers. See the privacy policy.

Is the app safe?

It is signed, and its SHA-256 fingerprint is published with each version. It updates from this site only.

What if Google Play is down when I upgrade?

Nothing is charged: we refuse rather than guess. Try again later.
