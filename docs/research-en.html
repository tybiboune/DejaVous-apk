MARKET STUDY · OCTOBER 3, 2026

Sell the night.
Not a card count.

Déjà Vous has a large catalogue, but several free rivals already advertise a thousand-plus prompts, and Togella advertises 11 modes. The more defensible difference is the complete experience: two people, private choices, changing game rhythms, stakes, and a finale.

What the market says

Volume is becoming a commodity.

Gottman advertises 1,000+ free cards; Lovewick 1,000+ questions; Togella 2,000+ questions and 11 modes. Déjà Vous’s 6,065 content units are real, but count alone is a fragile promise.

Physical games prove people pay for an occasion.

Conversation decks of roughly 135–200 cards sell for $20–35 in the US. The Adventure Challenge sells 50 scratch-off experiences for $49.99. The experience matters more than price per card.

A subscription is a poor first instinct.

Relationship apps sell daily routines, often through monthly or annual plans. Déjà Vous feels more like a night to replay and gift. Test a one-time experience before charging for continuous access.

The position to own

“A real game night for two that respects your pace and still surprises you.” The landing page opens with a secret choice and a door from Tonight. Visitors can play three mini-demos before seeing the comparison. This sells an experience first and uses the research to answer rational objections afterward.

Do not claim the world’s most cards or most modes. Publishers count different units, offers change, and some rivals are free. Nor should every rival be described as “just question cards”: Paired has guided journeys, Togella claims 11 modes, Esther Perel adds storytelling rules, and Adventure Challenge sells activities.

Installation may be a bigger conversion obstacle than price. A GitHub APK download adds a step and may trigger an Android permission prompt. The browser demos should build trust first. Measure APK clicks separately from completed installations; later, test a more familiar distribution channel if its content rules allow it.

A commercial bet worth testing

Keep today’s game free. Later, test a genuinely scripted, giftable evening at a one-time €19.90–24.90 for the couple, only after testing purchase intent and retention. This is a pricing hypothesis, not a current offer. One purchase should work for both partners; a printable invitation or gift code could make it as easy to give as a physical card deck.

Three experiments, in order

  1. An invitation, not just an APK link. Let one partner create a shareable “I have a surprise for us tonight” card. Measure whether creating it increases installs and completed nights.
  2. A one-price gift night. Test €19.90 and €24.90 intent pages before building checkout. A scripted experience, unique surprises, and a gift code must justify the price; extra locked questions alone will not.
  3. Nights designed with date venues. Pilot one private co-branded experience with a hotel, restaurant, or gift box. A partner can fund distribution without interrupting intimate play with ads. Measure completed nights before scaling.

For this landing page, track demo starts and completions, APK clicks, installs, and finished evenings separately, with privacy-respecting measurement. Without those signals, an “optimal” price is intuition.

  6. Lovify · French App Store ↗ — free + listed in-app purchases from €3.99. 1,000+ questions and 50+ topics; answer/guess play across two phones. The free share of its catalogue is not specified.
  7. Connected · official site ↗ — free access + listed $12.99/month or $77.99/year, 700+ prompts. Check-ins, assessments, and coaching; distinct game count unpublished.

  7. The Adventure Challenge Couples Edition ↗ — $49.99, 50 scratch-off adventures, not 50 questions. This shows what a giftable experience can command.

Déjà Vous: verified scope

Version 1.16.0 contains 3,150 Classic questions, 834 Same Brain prompts, 400 Fusion themes, 440 Two Truths prompts, 594 Rapid Fire pairs, and 647 Do You Know Me? statements: 6,065 content units. It also has 25 wildcards, 48 vouchers, and 36 final challenges. There are six standalone games (Classic plus five quick games); Tonight combines five stages and is not a seventh standalone game. Today it is free, bilingual, and playable on one phone or two nearby Android phones via Bluetooth, without accounts or ads. Saved answers stay on the devices; choices needed during two-phone play pass directly between partners.
