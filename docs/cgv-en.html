  1. Payment takes place on Google Play, with the payment methods of your Google account, under the Google Play Terms of Service. We never see your payment details.

Article 7How the full game opens

  1. The texts of the full game are encrypted in the app. They only open with a key.
  2. After the purchase, the app sends our license server, api.dejanous.love, the purchase token Google Play gave it. The server asks Google to confirm the purchase, then sends back the key. What is sent and kept is detailed in the privacy policy.
  3. The phone keeps the key. The full game then opens at every start, offline too.
  4. If the server does not answer at the time of purchase (no network, an outage), the app asks for the key again at every start until it gets it.
  5. The server can check that the request comes from the official app. A modified copy of the app gets no key.
