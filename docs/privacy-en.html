PRIVACY POLICY · OCTOBER 7, 2026

Your answers
stay yours.

Déjà Vous is a game for couples (18+). The publisher runs no server that receives your answers, your names or the contents of your “Book of us”. This page explains what stays on your phone, what travels, and what you can choose to back up.

On this website

The website sets no cookie and uses no third-party analytics. It anonymously counts a few events (page viewed, demo started or finished, install clicked, invitation created or opened, souvenir card created or shared, edition quiz answered, Google Play or offer clicked), with only the event name, the language and the page variant (a or b): no identifier, no IP address kept, counts aggregated per day. Counting is off when your browser asks not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track). Your evening code, your promise and the page variant stay in your browser (local storage); your demo answers never leave the page. An invitation link contains a card number and, if you chose to, your hidden answers (up to three choices) and your first name. A gift link holds the purchase code, your note and your first name after the “#”: browsers never send them to any server.

Buying Unleashed on the website

If you buy Unleashed on this site, payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe), which gives us your e-mail address and the amount. Our licence server stores only your e-mail, the order number and the code issued, so that we can resend it if you lose it and cancel it on a refund. To upgrade from the Google Play version to Unleashed you enter your Google Play order number, which we check with Google. No game answer and no content of the Book is ever sent. To ask for deletion, reply to the confirmation e-mail.

What the app does not do

No mandatory account, no ads, no advertising trackers, no usage analytics, no sale of data. The publisher receives neither your answers, your first names, your results, nor your Book.

What is stored on your phone

Settings, games in progress, the complicity history, vouchers won and “The Book of us” (answers kept, memories, words found together, rounds of “Two truths, one lie”) are stored locally, in the app’s private space. They are kept in two copies on the device to survive updates. Android’s automatic backup may include them in your phone’s Google backup, depending on your device settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them from the device.

Playing on two phones

Two-phone games use the direct connection between nearby phones (Bluetooth and Android’s “Nearby” service). The choices needed for the game travel directly between the two phones, without going through a publisher server. The app asks for Bluetooth, nearby devices and notification permissions only for this.

The Thread: a question sent by message

With “The Thread”, you answer a question on your phone, then the app prepares a message that you send with your own messaging app (WhatsApp, SMS, email or copy and paste). The link in that message holds the card number, your first name (and your gender, for French grammar), your answer and your guess, the other person’s when they reply, and the app’s edition. The answers are scrambled so they cannot be read at a glance before answering: this is not encryption, anyone who receives the link can recover them. This data sits after the “#” sign of the link: your browser never sends it to the website’s server, and the fil.html page only reads it on your phone to show the question and open the app. No Déjà Vous server receives or keeps these messages; they only travel through the messaging app you choose, under its own terms.

Internet connections

The app only contacts GitHub to check whether a new version exists (the public “update.json” file) and, when available, new cards. These requests carry no answers, names or Book content; like any Internet request, they expose your IP address to the service receiving them (GitHub). In the edition distributed on Google Play, buying the full game is handled by Google Play under its own policy. To activate a purchase, the app sends the Unleashed server the code and a random identifier of the phone, to enforce the two-phones-per-purchase limit; nothing else.

Optional backup in your Google Drive

If, and only if, you turn on “Back up with Google” in “The Book of us”, the app asks for the “drive.appdata” permission: a hidden folder of your Google Drive reserved for Déjà Vous. It saves a copy of your Book there so you can recover it after a reinstall or a new phone. The copy is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a random key that is itself protected by a password you choose (PBKDF2 derivation, 600,000 iterations); that password is never sent to, nor known by, the publisher or Google. Without it nobody can read the copy, and it cannot be recovered: if you forget it, you can set a new one from a phone that already has the backup turned on (it keeps the key in the app’s private space); if you no longer have that phone nor the password, the copy is permanently unreadable. Signing in to your Google account gives access to the encrypted file, not to its content. The app cannot access any other file in your Drive, does not read your e-mail address or profile, and the publisher has no access to that folder. The copy lives in your own Google account, under your Google terms. You can delete it at any time from the app (turn the backup off) or from your Google account settings (“Manage apps” > Déjà Vous > “Delete hidden app data”), and revoke access at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Déjà Vous’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: this data is only used to restore your Book and is neither transferred to third parties nor used for advertising.

Your rights

The data is on your devices or in your own Google account: you view, change and delete it yourself. The game is for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children.
