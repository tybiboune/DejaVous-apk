Déjà Nous is a game for couples (18+). Your answers, your first names and your “Book of us” stay on your phone: we never receive them. We only process what is needed to sell the full game, enforce its licence and answer you. This page says what, why, and for how long.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Philippe Delvigne EI, trading as “Déjà Nous Games”, 9 chemin Commanderie Saint-Jean-de-Malte, 13080 Aix-en-Provence, France.

For any question about your data: contact@dejanous.love.

In short

  • No mandatory account, no ads, no advertising trackers, no sale of data.
  • Your answers and “The Book of us” stay on your phone. They are not sent to any publisher server.
  • If you buy the full game, we keep your e-mail, your order, your code and a pseudonymous identifier of each of your phones.
  • The paid texts carry an invisible watermark that links them to your licence.
  • The website sets no cookie. It counts a few events anonymously.

What stays on your phone

Settings, games in progress, the complicity history, vouchers won and “The Book of us” (answers kept, memories, words found together, rounds of “Two truths, one lie”) are stored in the app’s private space, on your phone only. We have no access to them.

This data is kept in two copies on the device to survive updates. Android’s automatic backup may include it in your phone’s Google backup, depending on your settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it from the device.

The app has no mandatory account, no ads, no advertising trackers and no usage analytics.

Playing on two phones

Two-phone games use the direct connection between nearby phones (Bluetooth and Android’s “Nearby” service). The choices needed for the game go directly from one phone to the other, without any publisher server. The app asks for the Bluetooth, nearby devices and notification permissions only for this.

The Thread, invitations and gift links

With “The Thread”, you answer a question on your phone, then the app prepares a message that you send with your own messaging app (WhatsApp, SMS, e-mail or copy and paste). The link holds the card number, your first name (and your gender, for French grammar), your answer and your guess, the other person’s when they reply, and the app’s edition.

The answers are scrambled so they cannot be read at a glance. This is not encryption: anyone who receives the link can recover them. A Snap! “timed challenge” travels the same way (card numbers, first names and genders, true-or-false answers, time of each answer), never with the text of a card.

A gift link holds your note, your first name, the evening offered, the opening day if you chose one and, for the full game, the purchase code.

In Thread, challenge and gift links, this data sits after the “#” sign. Your browser never sends it to the website’s server: the page only reads it on the phone that opens it.

A website invitation link holds, in its address, a card number and, if you choose, your hidden answers (up to three choices) and your first name. Like any page address, it goes through the website’s host; the website keeps no record of it.

No Déjà Nous server keeps these messages. They go through the messaging app you choose, under its own terms.

On this website

No cookies. The public website sets no cookie and uses no third-party analytics or advertising service.

Local storage. Your evening code, your promise, the page variant (a or b), your display preferences and, for the length of the visit, the first names of the demo stay in your browser. Your demo answers never leave the page.

Anonymous counting. The website counts a few events (page viewed, demo started or finished, install clicked, invitation created or opened, souvenir card created or shared, edition quiz answered, Google Play or offer clicked). It only sends the event name, the language and the page variant. Counting is off when your browser asks not to be tracked (Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track).

Purpose
Knowing which pages and steps are used, to improve the website.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in measuring the audience, without identifying anyone.
Data
Event name, language, page variant, added up per day. To limit abuse, the server computes a fingerprint of your IP address each day; it never writes the address itself.
Retention
Daily counters: 400 days, then deleted automatically. The address fingerprint: 24 hours.

Hosting. The website is served by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare receives your IP address to send you the pages and protect the website against attacks.

Anti-robot check. The purchase and code-resend forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to block automated submissions (spam). It looks at technical signals from your browser (IP address, browser characteristics, behaviour on the page); it is not used for advertising and sets no tracking cookie. We only receive a “verified person” token.

Buying the full game

You buy “Déjà Nous Unleashed” on this website or from the app. You give your e-mail address and confirm that you are 18 or over. Payment then takes place on Stripe’s page.

Stripe gives us your e-mail and the payment status. We never see your card number. Our licence server then creates your code and sends it by e-mail.

Purpose
Concluding and performing the sale: delivering the code, resending it if you lose it (“Find my code”), handling a refund, a withdrawal or a bank dispute.
Legal basis
Performance of the sales contract. Afterwards, our accounting and tax obligations.
Data
E-mail address, language, product bought, payment session number and Stripe payment reference, licence code and its identifier, date of purchase. For an upgrade from the Google Play edition, if that offer is available: the Google Play order number, which we check with Google.
Retention
10 years from the purchase [À CONFIRMER], the retention period for accounting records. The licence has no end date: this data is also used to resend your code during that period.

The payment page is hosted by Stripe, which applies its own rules there, cookies included. Stripe also processes some data on its own behalf, for example against fraud, under its privacy policy.

Activating the full game on your phones

One purchase covers two phones. To activate a phone, then renew its right to play every 14 days, the app contacts our server api.dejanous.love.

It only sends: the code, an identifier of the phone, the fingerprint of the certificate that signs the app (to check that it is the official app) and the name of its edition’s card vault. It sends neither your name, nor your e-mail, nor your answers.

The phone identifier is pseudonymous. It is computed on the phone, from the app’s own Android identifier, with a one-way function. It cannot be used to find the phone or its owner.

Purpose
Applying the limit of two phones per purchase, renewing the 14-day right and delivering the key that opens the texts of the full game.
Legal basis
Performance of the contract (the licence for two phones).
Data
For each licence: the pseudonymous identifier of each activated phone (two at most) and its activation date, and the date of the last phone replacement (one per month).
Retention
A phone is deleted as soon as you remove it (“Remove”) or replace it. Otherwise its data is deleted together with the order data. The date of the last replacement is deleted after 30 days.

Switched-off codes. When a code is switched off (refund, bank dispute, abuse), its identifier goes into a public list that the app downloads at most every 6 hours. This identifier is a random 8-character number; the list holds no e-mail and no other data. It is kept as long as the switch-off must apply, and at most as long as the order data [À CONFIRMER].

Google Play edition. Buying the full game is handled by Google Play, under its own policy. To open the texts of the full game, the app sends our server the Google Play purchase token, and the server asks Google to confirm it. We only keep a fingerprint of this token for 24 hours, so as not to ask Google each time the app opens.

The watermark of the paid texts

Every copy of the full game is personal. When the paid texts open on your phone, the app adds a mark specific to your licence, in two ways:

  • an invisible signature, made of zero-width characters, placed just before the final punctuation of each text;
  • on a few cards, a number that changes from one licence to another, without changing the meaning of the card.

The mark is your licence identifier (direct edition), or the start of a fingerprint of your Google Play purchase token (Google Play edition). It is added on your phone: nothing is sent for it. It changes neither the meaning of the cards nor the way you play.

If texts of the game are spread (file, screenshot, copy of the app, copying by hand), we can read the mark and find the original licence, and so the order and the buyer’s e-mail address. This is what article 10 of the terms of sale provides for.

Purpose
Protecting our texts against copying, enforcing the licence and defending our rights.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in protecting our works and fighting piracy, and performance of the licence contract.
Data
The licence identifier written into the texts. We only link it to an order when a spread text is found.
Retention
The mark stays in the texts shown on your phone. The analysis of a spread text is kept for the length of the procedure, and until the end of any dispute [À CONFIRMER].

Server security and fighting abuse

To slow down mass attempts, the server counts requests per IP address for one hour (payment, code, activation, Google Play edition key). It also counts “Find my code” requests per e-mail address for 24 hours. These counters delete themselves.

Purpose
Protecting the service against abuse and attacks.
Legal basis
Our legitimate interest in securing the service.
Data
IP address or e-mail address, and a number of requests.
Retention
1 hour for the IP address, 24 hours for the e-mail address.

Our server writes no log of your requests [À CONFIRMER]. Cloudflare, which hosts it, processes IP addresses to route requests and block attacks, under its own policy.

E-mails

E-mails we send. We only write to you about your purchase: the e-mail that delivers your code after payment, and the same e-mail if you ask “Find my code”. They are sent through our provider Resend. We send no newsletter and no advertising. Resend keeps a record of what is sent under its own policy.

If the same Google Play order is paid twice, the server sends us an internal alert with the order number, so that we refund you the second payment.

E-mails we receive. Messages sent to contact@dejanous.love are routed by Cloudflare to our inbox. We only use them to answer you.

Purpose
Delivering the code, answering your questions, complaints and requests (withdrawal, rights over your data).
Legal basis
Performance of the contract, and our legitimate interest in answering you.
Data
Your e-mail address, the content of our exchanges.
Retention
3 years after our last exchange [À CONFIRMER]. An exchange about an order (withdrawal, complaint) is kept as long as the order.

The app’s other connections

The app contacts GitHub to check whether a new version exists (the public “update.json” file) and to receive new cards. It also downloads the list of switched-off codes from api.dejanous.love (direct edition). These requests carry no answers, names or Book content. Like any Internet request, they show your IP address to the service receiving them.

The app’s installation file and its updates are downloaded from GitHub.

Optional backup in your Google Drive

If, and only if, you turn on “Back up with Google” in “The Book of us”, the app asks for the “drive.appdata” permission: a hidden folder of your Google Drive reserved for Déjà Nous. It saves a copy of your Book there, so you can recover it after a reinstall or a new phone.

The copy is encrypted on your phone before it is sent, with a random key that is itself protected by a password you choose (PBKDF2 derivation, 600,000 iterations). That password is never sent, neither to the publisher nor to Google. Without it, nobody can read the copy. It cannot be recovered: if you forget it, you can set a new one from a phone that already has the backup turned on (it keeps the key in the app’s private space). Without that phone and without the password, the copy is permanently unreadable.

Signing in to your Google account gives access to the encrypted file, not to its content. The app cannot access any other file in your Drive, does not read your e-mail address or profile, and the publisher has no access to that folder. The copy lives in your own Google account, under your Google terms.

You can delete it at any time from the app (turn the backup off) or from your Google account settings (“Manage apps” > Déjà Nous > “Delete hidden app data”), and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Déjà Nous’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: this data is only used to restore your Book and is neither transferred to third parties nor used for advertising.

These transfers are covered by the safeguards provided for by the GDPR: the EU–US Data Privacy Framework when the provider has joined it, or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses [À CONFIRMER]. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to object to processing based on our legitimate interest, to ask for it to be restricted, and to receive the data you gave us in a common format (portability).

How to do it. Write to contact@dejanous.love, if possible from the e-mail address of your purchase. We answer within one month. We may ask you to prove your identity if we are in doubt.

The limits. We keep what the law requires us to keep, such as accounting records. If you ask us to erase your e-mail, we will no longer be able to resend your code.

The data that stays on your phone or in your Google account is yours to view, change and delete: we have no access to it.

Complaint. If you think your rights are not respected, you can lodge a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority: www.cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France.
